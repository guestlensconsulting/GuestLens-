The GuestLens Ten · No. 01

Seven places for a Christmas party around Grafton Street

Seven places, one thing each. Ordered by GuestLens Score.

Christmas parties around Grafton Street and South William Street, Dublin 2. We looked for venues that say on their own websites that they host groups, set menus or private rooms, then scored each one by our published method. Check each venue for this season's menus and packages.

01 St Stephen's Green

Peploe's

9.0 Highly recommended · Desk rated

Slow-cooked veal ragu with rigatoni, which it calls a guest favourite, served in a Georgian-listed building.

Its website lists a set Christmas lunch menu, a set Christmas dinner menu and corporate lunches and dining.

Closed on Sundays. Phone reservations are taken 12pm to 10pm, Monday to Saturday.

Badge (square) · Badge (story)

02 Drury Street

SOLE Seafood & Grill

9.0 Highly recommended · Desk rated

The Captain's Table, which it calls iconic: an intimate yet lively setting within the main restaurant.

Its website invites Christmas lunch and dinner celebrations and says the Captain's Table suits groups of up to 18 guests.

Books online. Lunch from Thursday to Sunday; evenings only from Monday to Wednesday.

Badge (square) · Badge (story)

03 Exchequer Street

Fallon & Byrne

8.9 Highly recommended · Desk rated

Three rooms under one roof: The Wine Cellar, The Dining Room and The Exchange.

Its website matches a room to the party: the Wine Cellar for intimate gatherings, the Dining Room for feasts and The Exchange for lively parties.

Christmas parties book through its own party booking page.

Badge (square) · Badge (story)

04 Fade Street

Fade Street Social

8.8 Highly recommended · Desk rated

A Sunday roast of tender roast beef with a giant Yorkshire pudding, and a rooftop terrace.

Its website has a Christmas page, corporate bookings and group menus for celebrations, with an events team to plan them.

Group and event enquiries go to its events team by email; table bookings are online.

Badge (square) · Badge (story)

05 Duke Street

Davy Byrnes

8.4 Recommended · Desk rated

In its own words, a literary, heritage and cosmopolitan pub, and an Art Deco pub at that.

It publishes set menus online and takes bookings for groups of seven or more by phone.

Tables for up to six can be booked online.

Badge (square) · Badge (story)

06 Balfe Street

The Westbury

8.2 Recommended · Desk rated

Afternoon Tea in The Gallery, which the hotel calls legendary.

It lists the Grafton Suite and the Trinity Suite as event venues, with event menus on its website.

The hotel is also home to WILDE, Balfes and The Sidecar cocktail bar.

Badge (square) · Badge (story)

07 South Great George's Street

San Lorenzo's

8.2 Recommended · Desk rated

The Brunch of Champions at weekends, in smart New York inspired interiors.

Its website says the low-lit, partitioned layout offers intimacy for groups.

Books online through OpenTable, or by phone or email.

Badge (square) · Badge (story)

How we score

The GuestLens Score is out of 10: guest reputation across public sources (4), a specialty stated by the venue and checked at source (3), and our own check of booking, menus and ordering online (3). Only venues scoring 8.0 or more are listed. Desk rated means we have not visited yet. Featuring is free and independent. No venue pays or is asked to pay. Spotted an error?
